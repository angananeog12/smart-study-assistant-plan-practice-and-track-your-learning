## Problem Statement

Students often need to manage different study activities such as planning their study time, practicing questions, recording marks, and identifying subjects that require more attention.

Managing these activities separately can make it difficult to keep track of overall academic progress. The Smart Study Assistant is designed to bring these basic study-related activities together in one simple Python application.

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to develop a Python-based application that helps students:

- Organize their study activities
- Plan their study schedule
- Practice through test
- Record and track academic performance
- Receive simple study recommendations based on their performance

## Target Users

The primary users of this application are students who want a simple tool to organize their study activities and monitor their academic progress.

## Scope of the Project

The project focuses on basic study management and academic practice. The application includes:

1. Student information management
2. Study planning
3. Test and practice activities
4. Marks and performance tracking
5. Basic study recommendations

## High-Level Features

### Student Details

The application allows students to enter and manage the information required by the system.

### Study Planner

The planner helps students organize their subjects and study activities.

### Test and Practice

The test functionality allows students to practice questions and check their performance.

### Performance Tracking

The application allows students to record marks and view their academic performance.

### Recommendations

The recommendation feature provides simple study suggestions based on the student's recorded performance.

## Output

The expected outcome is a functional Python application that provides students with a simple way to plan their studies,
practice through test ,track academic performance, and receive basic study recommendations.
The project also demonstrates the practical application of Python programming concepts and modular program design.
